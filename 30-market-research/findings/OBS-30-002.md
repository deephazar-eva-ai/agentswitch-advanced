# OBS-30-002: On Suryodaya, some activities have a word instead of a date in their due date

Seat 30 observation. On 26 Nov 2026 the instructor confirmed in answer 14 that these malformed due dates are test-data quirks.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Title | On Suryodaya, some activities have a word instead of a date in their due date |
| Seat and login | 30, the seat 30 login. First seen with the seat 31 login, before seat 30 had one |
| System | Suryodaya. Keystone is not affected |
| Date and time (IST) | 24 Nov 2026, between about 9:45 and 10:15 am |
| Severity | Observation |
| Repeats | Every time: the same records on every read on 24 Nov, with both logins |
| Status | Closed on 26 Nov 2026 (instructor answer 14) |

**Steps** (the exact tool name and request text are in the version sent to the course team)
1. Log in on Suryodaya with the seat 30 login (or the seat 31 login).
2. List all activities, with the read-only list tool, paging to the end.
3. For each activity, check that the due date is empty or a calendar date (`YYYY-MM-DD`).

**Expected**
Every activity's due date is either empty or a calendar date, as on Keystone, where every activity has a calendar date.

**Actual**
- On Suryodaya, many activities have a short word in the due date instead of a date. The words are all the same length, and some end part-way through a word, so they look like truncated text.
- All of them are open meetings (not done, not cancelled), each linked to a different account and none to a deal. None has a due time or a creator, and all were created on the same day in September 2026.
- The other date fields we read are fine: deal close dates are calendar dates on both systems.

We changed nothing; every request was a read.

**Evidence**
The exact request text, the count and the record ids are sent to the course team. Nothing is attached here, so no platform data is included.

**How the brief handles it**

The activity stays open, shows no due date, and never counts as overdue. Each shown deal with one or more such activities gets one data issue, “account activity: due date is not a date”. A genuinely empty due date is valid. The report and the deal explanation use the same issue text.
